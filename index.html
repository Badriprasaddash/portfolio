About Badri Prasad Dash

Academic Biography & Focus

Dr. Badri Prasad Dash is a dedicated scholar and researcher deeply immersed in the study of International Geopolitics, global governance, and contemporary foreign policy. With foundational academic roots from Utkal University and advanced post-graduate research degrees (M.Phil. and Ph.D.) from the historic University of Madras, Chennai, Dr. Dash brings a rigorous analytical methodology to understanding complex geopolitical affairs.

His academic inquiries explore how territorial dynamics, maritime security, multilateral alignments, and national interests intersect to shape foreign diplomacy across Asia, the Indo-Pacific, and global corridors of influence.

Strategic Analysis

Critical assessment of international conflicts, border treaties, and bilateral relations.

Institutional Research

In-depth study of regional bodies like ASEAN, QUAD, SCO, and multilateral treaties.

Pedagogy & Dialogue

Research Interests & Expertise

Core thematic pillars within International Geopolitics and Global Strategic Studies.

International Geopolitics

Analyzing tectonic shifts in contemporary global power distribution, multi-polarity, hegemonic transitions, and the reconfiguration of diplomatic alliances.

  • Great Power Competition
  • Eurasian & Indo-Pacific Geopolitics
  • Balance of Power Theories

Maritime Security & Indo-Pacific

Investigation into sea lines of communication (SLOCs), choke points, maritime diplomacy, and island diplomacy across the Indian and Pacific Oceans.

  • Straits of Malacca, Hormuz & Bab-el-Mandeb
  • Quad & Naval Multilateralism
  • Blue Economy & Ocean Governance

Foreign Policy & Diplomacy

Critically evaluating bilateral relationships, neighborhood strategies, strategic autonomy, and proactive summit diplomacy in a fractured global order.

  • India's Act East & Neighborhood First
  • Strategic Autonomy in Multilateralism
  • Track 1.5 & Track 2 Diplomatic Channels

Regional Security Architectures

Investigating conflict prevention, border management, counter-terrorism coalitions, and defense frameworks across South and Central Asia.

  • Cross-Border Dynamics & Ceasefire Systems
  • SAARC, BIMSTEC, & ASEAN Security Forums
  • Nuclear Deterrence & Crisis Stability

Geoeconomics & Resource Politics

Intersections between energy dependency, critical minerals, transnational infrastructure corridors, and economic statecraft.

  • Belt and Road vs. IMEC Initiatives
  • Energy Security & Green Transition Politics
  • Supply Chain Reshoring & Sanctions

Global Governance & Multilateralism

Evolution of international regimes, reform of the United Nations Security Council, and the rise of Global South representation in G20 and BRICS.

  • UN System & Institutional Reform

  • South-South Cooperation Frameworks
